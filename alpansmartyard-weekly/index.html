►
Metrics to Watch — CTR of 0.32% and CVR of 5.11% tell an encouraging story — shoppers who click are converting at a healthy rate, confirming strong product-market fit and listing quality. ►

Shifting budget toward top-converting search terms while testing new ad creatives can help push ROAS above the 2.0x threshold and bring ACOS into a more sustainable range.
►
Actions This Week — Audit sponsored campaigns to identify high-spend, low-conversion keywords and reallocate that budget toward proven top performers to directly address the 51.6% ACOS. Test fresh ad creative angles — such as seasonal or use-case-driven imagery — to improve CTR beyond the current 0.32% and drive more qualified clicks into the funnel. With Monday Nov 2 confirmed as the peak sales day, front-load ad budgets toward early-week scheduling in Week 46 to replicate and build on that performance pattern.
