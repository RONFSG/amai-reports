Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Alpan/SmartYard generated $1,828 in total sales across 49 units during Week 47, marking a solid +4.4% week-over-week gain with Thursday November 19 standing out as the peak revenue day. A ROAS of 1.26x alongside a TACoS of 39.4% reflects that ad spend is currently consuming a significant share of total revenue, signaling that organic contribution still has room to grow relative to paid performance. With positive momentum heading into Week 48, the foundation is in place to build on this upward trend as the holiday shopping window accelerates.
►
Metrics to Watch — CTR of 0.51% and CVR of 3.76% together show that shoppers who do engage with the ads are converting at a healthy rate, which is an encouraging sign of solid product-market fit in the listings. The priority now is strengthening that top-of-funnel click volume so the strong CVR has more traffic to work with. Monitoring CTR daily in Week 48 will help identify whether ad creative adjustments are gaining traction and driving incremental unit volume toward 55+ units.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — The current ACOS of 79.3% against $720 in ad spend generating $908 in PPC revenue highlights a meaningful opportunity to sharpen campaign targeting and reduce wasted spend on low-converting keywords. Shifting budget toward the top-performing keywords and dayparting spend around peak days like Thursday can improve return on each dollar deployed. Even a modest ACOS reduction toward the 60–65% range in Week 48 would meaningfully improve overall profitability and push ROAS closer to 1.5x.
►
Actions This Week — Audit the active keyword list and pause any terms running above 90% ACOS for three or more consecutive days, reallocating that budget toward proven converters to tighten efficiency. Test a refreshed ad creative variation on the top-performing ASIN to improve the 0.51% CTR, with the goal of reaching 0.65% or better by end of Week 48. Capitalize on the Thursday peak-day pattern by increasing bid adjustments mid-week to maximize impression share during the highest-converting window of the week.
