Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Week 32 closed with $433 in total sales, reflecting a 56.2% week-over-week decline that signals a reset moment worth addressing head-on. A ROAS of 0.31x alongside a TACoS of 61.9% indicates that ad spend is currently outpacing returns, meaning organic sales contribution remains limited relative to what PPC is costing to drive. Heading into Week 33, Saturday's peak performance on August 8 gives us a behavioral signal to build around as we sharpen targeting and timing.
►
Metrics to Watch — A CVR of 2.05% shows that shoppers who do land on the listing are converting at a meaningful rate, which confirms the product itself is compelling once it gets in front of the right audience. The 0.37% CTR from ads is the area with the clearest runway for improvement, and even a modest lift there could meaningfully increase PPC revenue above the current $77. Keeping a close eye on CVR stability while working to lift CTR will be the combination that drives compounding gains in the coming weeks.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $257 in ad spend generating $77 in PPC revenue and an ACOS of 26.2%, the efficiency gap is primarily a volume and click-quality challenge rather than a conversion problem. The constructive path forward is reallocating budget toward the keywords and placements that drove Saturday's peak, reducing wasted impressions on lower-intent traffic. Tightening bid strategy around proven performers will stretch the same $257 further and bring ROAS back toward a healthier threshold.
►
Actions This Week — Pull Saturday August 8 search term and placement data to identify what drove peak-day performance and prioritize those inputs in campaign targeting for Week 33. Test new ad creative angles — such as refreshed headline copy or lifestyle-focused imagery — to lift the 0.37% CTR and bring more qualified traffic into the funnel. Set a TACoS improvement target of 50% or below for Week 33 by pausing or reducing bids on keywords with high spend and zero conversions over the past 14 days.
