►
Metrics to Watch — CTR at 0.23% and CVR at 3.32% tell an interesting story — shoppers who click are converting at a healthy rate, meaning the product page is doing its job. Keeping a close eye on CVR stability as ad volume scales will be key to sustaining this growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $1,215 in ad spend driving $1,390 in PPC revenue, the ACOS at 87.4% signals that paid sales are currently running at a loss on a per-ad-dollar basis, which is worth addressing as overall volume grows. The gap between ACOS and profitability targets is an opportunity to tighten keyword bids, pause underperforming targets, and shift budget toward the placements and terms delivering the strongest returns. Incremental improvements here will compound meaningfully heading into the high-traffic holiday weeks ahead.
►
Actions This Week — Audit keyword-level ACOS data and reduce bids on terms exceeding target thresholds while reallocating that budget to top-converting search terms that are driving efficient revenue. Test new ad creative variations — including updated main image angles or lifestyle-focused secondary images — to improve CTR from the current 0.23% and bring more qualified clicks into the funnel. Continue monitoring daily sales pacing closely, using Sunday as a benchmark day, and prepare budget flexibility to capitalize on any demand spikes in Week 46.
