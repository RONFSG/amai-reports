Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Total sales came in at $3,147 across 39 units sold, reflecting a 22.4% week-over-week decline with Wednesday, November 11 standing out as the strongest individual day of the period. A ROAS of 0.70x and TACoS of 24.6% signal that ad spend is currently outpacing the revenue it directly generates, indicating an opportunity to sharpen targeting and improve return before the holiday stretch. With peak shopping days ahead in mid-to-late November, there is real runway to recover momentum and push weekly performance back above recent baselines.
►
Metrics to Watch — A CVR of 2.39% shows that shoppers who click are converting at a reasonable rate, meaning the product listing is doing meaningful work once traffic arrives. The focus now is on feeding that conversion engine with higher-quality, more relevant traffic to amplify what is already working. Monitoring CTR closely at 0.15% will be key, as even modest improvements there can compound quickly into stronger unit velocity heading into the holiday window.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $773 in ad spend generating $540 in PPC revenue and an ACOS of 143.2%, the current campaign structure is delivering below its potential and warrants a focused audit this week. Reallocating budget toward the best-performing keywords and pausing or reducing bids on high-spend, low-return terms will help bring ACOS into a more sustainable range. Tightening bid discipline now positions the account to scale more efficiently as organic demand naturally increases through late November.
►
Actions This Week — Conduct a keyword-level review of all active campaigns and reduce bids by 20–30% on any keyword with ACOS exceeding 100% and no recent conversions to redirect that spend toward proven performers. Test fresh ad creative with updated copy and imagery to address the low 0.15% CTR, as improved click-through rates will directly reduce effective ACOS and stretch the existing $773 budget further. Set a daily spend cap review mid-week to ensure budget is not exhausting early in the day, preserving visibility during peak evening shopping hours ahead of the Thanksgiving demand surge.
