Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Matter of Fact generated $7,267 in total sales across 124 units sold during Week 47, marking a remarkable +82.5% week-over-week surge with Thursday November 19 standing out as the peak revenue day. A 2.18x ROAS paired with a 20.4% TACoS reflects a campaign structure that is driving meaningful paid volume while maintaining reasonable overhead relative to total sales. With this level of momentum heading into the final stretch of November, the brand is well-positioned to carry that velocity into Week 48 and capitalize on continued holiday shopping intent.
►
Metrics to Watch — A conversion rate of 8.33% is a strong signal that shoppers are arriving with real purchase intent, and protecting that rate as traffic scales will be a key driver of continued growth. The 0.18% CTR on ad placements is the most immediate lever available — incremental improvements here can unlock meaningfully more revenue from the same ad spend. Keeping a close eye on both CTR and CVR together will help ensure that volume gains next week are efficient and sustainable.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — The $1,481 in ad spend delivered $3,231 in PPC revenue at a 45.8% ACoS, which is worth examining in the context of margin targets to determine the optimal efficiency threshold going forward. That said, the overall TACoS of 20.4% confirms that organic sales are carrying a healthy share of total revenue, softening the impact of paid costs across the full business. Tightening keyword bids on underperforming targets while reallocating toward top converters is the clearest path to improving ACoS without sacrificing the growth trajectory.
►
Actions This Week — Audit sponsored campaigns to identify keywords with high spend and low conversion, then shift that budget toward the terms most responsible for driving the Week 47 surge. Test new ad creative variations to build on the strong Thursday peak and determine whether different messaging or imagery can lift CTR beyond the current 0.18% baseline. With +82.5% WoW growth already in hand, focus this week on locking in structural efficiencies that make Week 48 performance repeatable rather than relying on momentum alone.
