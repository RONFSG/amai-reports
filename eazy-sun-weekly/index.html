►
Metrics to Watch — CVR at 9.28% is a genuine strength and shows that shoppers who engage with the listing are converting at a healthy rate, making it a reliable foundation to scale traffic into. CTR at 0.43% from ads presents the clearest opportunity for growth, as even a modest lift in click-through rate would drive more qualified traffic into a listing that is already converting well. Keeping a close eye on the Tuesday demand spike each week will help identify whether mid-week momentum is a repeatable trend worth planning campaigns around.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $709 in ad spend generated $987 in PPC revenue, producing a 1.39x ROAS and an ACOS of 71.8%, which indicates the current ad investment is working but has meaningful room to become more efficient. TACoS at 30.0% is the most important efficiency signal right now, and reducing it toward the 20–25% range will require either growing total organic sales or tightening spend on underperforming targets. The constructive path forward is to reallocate budget away from keywords with high spend and low conversion, and concentrate dollars on the terms already driving the strongest return.
►
Actions This Week — Audit current PPC campaigns and pause or reduce bids on keywords with ACOS significantly above 71.8%, redirecting that budget toward top-converting search terms to improve overall ad efficiency. Test new ad creative variations — including refreshed main image copy or lifestyle angles in Sponsored Brand ads — to address the 0.43% CTR and generate more clicks from the existing impression volume. Plan a mid-week bid boost or budget increase targeting Tuesday through Wednesday to capitalize on the confirmed peak-day demand pattern and maximize revenue during the highest-converting window.
