►
Weekly Highlights — Reviv3 brought in $3,190 in total sales across 39 units sold during Week 35, reflecting a 16.2% week-over-week dip that signals an opportunity to sharpen our approach heading into late August. A ROAS of 1.64x against a TACoS of 29.4% shows that ad spend is consuming a significant share of total revenue, meaning organic sales velocity still has meaningful room to grow relative to paid investment. With Tuesday Aug 25 confirming strong mid-week demand, we have a clear timing signal to build momentum and target a sales recovery above $3,800 in Week 36.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR held strong at 13.40%, which tells us that shoppers who land on the listing are converting at a healthy clip — a real foundation to build on. The focus now shifts to CTR at 0.27%, where even a modest improvement in ad creative engagement can meaningfully expand the top of the funnel without requiring additional budget. Strengthening click volume into that 13%+ CVR engine is one of the highest-leverage moves available right now.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $939 in ad spend generating $1,537 in PPC revenue, the 61.1% ACoS reflects an efficiency gap that is very addressable through smarter bid and keyword management. Prioritizing spend toward the search terms and targeting types that are already driving conversions will help compress ACoS while protecting revenue. The goal entering Week 36 is to hold or grow PPC revenue while pulling ACoS meaningfully closer to the 45–50% range through disciplined optimization.
►
Actions This Week — Audit all active campaigns and pause or reduce bids on high-spend, low-converting search terms to immediately improve ACoS and protect margin. Launch a structured ad creative test with two to three headline or image variations to address the 0.27% CTR and drive more qualified traffic into the listing. Align increased bid support around Tuesday and mid-week dayparting windows based on the Week 35 peak day data to capitalize on proven demand timing.
