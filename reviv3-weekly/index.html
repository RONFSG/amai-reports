Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Reviv3 brought in $2,953 in total sales across Week 42 (October 11–17), reflecting a 42.7% week-over-week decline with Sunday, October 11 carrying the strongest single-day performance. A 1.69x ROAS alongside a 37.7% TACoS signals that paid traffic is driving a meaningful share of revenue but that organic sales volume needs to close the gap to bring blended efficiency into a healthier range. Heading into Week 43, the Sunday peak offers a clear targeting signal to front-load ad budgets and capitalize on proven high-conversion windows.
►
Metrics to Watch — The 15.13% conversion rate is a genuine bright spot, confirming that shoppers who reach the listing are highly motivated to buy, which gives the brand a strong foundation to scale from. CTR at 0.14% on the ad side is the metric that deserves the most attention, as improving click-through will multiply the impact of that strong CVR and drive more revenue without requiring a budget increase. Monitoring CTR response as creative and targeting adjustments are made this week will be the clearest indicator of whether traffic quality and volume are trending in the right direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — The $1,114 ad spend generated $1,883 in PPC revenue at a 59.2% ACOS, which reflects a campaign structure that is working but has clear room to improve its efficiency ratio. Tightening bids on underperforming keywords and reallocating that budget toward the placements and terms driving the strongest returns can meaningfully compress ACOS over the next two to three weeks. A targeted bid strategy review this week positions the brand to enter November — peak Q4 traffic season — with a leaner, higher-performing ad structure.
►
Actions This Week — Audit keyword-level ACOS data and pause or reduce bids on any terms exceeding a 70% ACOS threshold, redirecting that spend toward top-converting placements. Test new ad creative variations to address the 0.14% CTR, focusing on headline copy and primary image angles that more directly communicate the product's core benefit to drive higher click volume. Increase daily budgets on Sunday to capture the demonstrated peak-day demand, and set up dayparting or budget rules to ensure ads remain active and competitive throughout that high-intent window.
