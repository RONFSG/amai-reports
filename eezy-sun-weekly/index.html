Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Eezy Sun brought in $1,893 in total sales across 114 units sold during Week 33, reflecting an 18% week-over-week decline with Sunday August 9 standing out as the strongest individual day. A ROAS of 1.12x alongside a TACoS of 41.4% signals that ad spend is consuming a significant share of total revenue, leaving limited margin headroom and indicating the business is currently in a growth-investment phase. Carrying Sunday's momentum forward and tightening ad efficiency will be the two levers most likely to stabilize and rebuild sales volume heading into Week 34.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR at 6.52% is a genuine bright spot, showing that shoppers who land on the listing are converting at a healthy rate, which means the product and listing content are doing their job. The area to focus energy on is CTR at 0.57%, since improving ad click-through rate would feed more qualified traffic into that already-strong conversion funnel. Prioritizing CTR growth through sharper ad creative and headline testing represents one of the highest-leverage opportunities available right now.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $784 in ad spend generated $875 in PPC revenue, producing a ROAS of 1.12x and an ACOS of 89.6%, which reflects a campaign structure that is capturing sales but not yet doing so profitably. The constructive path forward is to identify which keywords or targets are driving spend without returns and reallocate that budget toward the placements already producing conversions. Small, deliberate bid adjustments combined with pausing low-performing targets can meaningfully improve ACOS without sacrificing the sales velocity the account needs.
►
Actions This Week — Audit all active ad targets and pause any keyword or ASIN target with high spend and zero or near-zero conversions over the past 14 days, redirecting that budget toward proven converters. Test fresh ad creative variations, including updated main image angles or headline copy, to address the 0.57% CTR and pull more clicks from existing impressions. Use Sunday as an anchor day by ensuring budgets are not capped early in the day, capturing peak traffic and replicating the conditions that made August 9 the week's strongest performer.
