Tightening bid discipline and pausing underperforming ad groups this week can begin shifting ROAS upward from 1.13x toward a healthier range within the next two to three weeks.
►
Actions This Week — Audit all active campaigns and pause or reduce bids on any keywords running above 80% ACOS to redirect spend toward top-performing terms with room to scale.
