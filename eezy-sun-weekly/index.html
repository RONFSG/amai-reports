►
Weekly Highlights — Eezy Sun generated $1,651 in total sales across Week 34 (August 16–22), reflecting a 6.2% dip week-over-week as summer demand begins its seasonal shift. A ROAS of 1.32x alongside a TACoS of 47.2% signals that ad spend is carrying a significant portion of revenue, meaning organic visibility still has room to grow relative to paid activity. With Friday August 21 confirmed as the peak sales day, there is a clear opportunity to enter Week 35 with stronger mid-to-late week bid positioning to capture that same momentum.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR at 5.07% is a genuine bright spot, confirming that shoppers who reach the listing are converting at a healthy rate, which speaks well to listing quality and product-market fit. The priority now is pulling CTR up from its current 0.51% so that stronger conversion rate can work against a larger pool of clicks. Monitoring CTR response to any ad creative adjustments this week will be the clearest early signal of whether Week 35 is trending in the right direction.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $779 in ad spend driving $1,027 in PPC revenue, the campaign is generating returns but an ACOS of 75.8% indicates the cost to acquire each sale through ads remains high relative to margin. The constructive path forward is tightening bids on underperforming keywords while protecting spend on terms that contributed to Friday's peak-day performance. Incremental ACOS improvement of even 5–8 points over the next two weeks would meaningfully strengthen the overall profitability profile.
►
Actions This Week — Audit keyword-level ACOS data and pause or reduce bids on any terms exceeding 90% ACOS to redirect budget toward higher-efficiency placements. Test refreshed ad creative with updated imagery or copy angles to address the 0.51% CTR, as stronger click-through will compound the existing 5.07% CVR advantage. Apply increased bids on Thursday through Saturday to front-load spend into the proven peak-day window and give Week 35 the best chance to reverse the WoW decline.
