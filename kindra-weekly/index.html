►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra closed Week 32 with $32,014 in total sales, marking a solid +3.4% week-over-week gain and continuing a positive trajectory heading into mid-August. With Sunday August 2nd serving as the peak sales day, early-week momentum is a pattern worth capitalizing on as we build into Week 33.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR of 12.70% is a genuine strength and signals that shoppers who reach the listing are converting at a healthy rate, giving the team a strong foundation to work with. CTR at 0.49% on the ad side presents a clear opportunity — even a modest lift here could meaningfully increase qualified traffic without requiring additional spend. Keeping a close eye on how CTR responds to any creative or copy adjustments will be key to unlocking the next layer of growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — $9,536 in ad spend generated $16,235 in PPC revenue, and while an ACOS of 58.7% is elevated, the strong CVR confirms the core offer is resonating once shoppers engage. The priority this week is identifying which campaigns or targets are driving the highest conversion rates and reallocating budget toward those to bring ACOS into a more efficient range. Tightening spend distribution rather than pulling back overall gives the best chance to protect revenue while improving returns.
►
Actions This Week — Audit PPC campaigns to identify and reduce or pause spend on keywords with high ACOS and low conversion, redirecting that budget toward proven top performers to improve overall efficiency. Test new ad creative variations — including updated imagery or headline copy — to address the 0.49% CTR and drive more clicks from existing impressions. Given Sunday's peak performance in Week 32, consider scheduling budget increases on Saturdays and Sundays in Week 33 to capture early-week demand before it peaks.
