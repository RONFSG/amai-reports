►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra closed Week 46 with $45,849 in total sales across 854 units, marking a solid +4.3% week-over-week gain. With Monday Nov 9 establishing the week's peak and positive WoW momentum in place, Week 47 is well-positioned to build on this trajectory heading deeper into the November selling season.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR of 16.26% is a genuine bright spot, signaling that shoppers landing on Kindra's listings are converting at a strong rate. CTR of 0.38% from ads represents the clearest lever available — even a modest lift here would drive more qualified traffic into that high-converting funnel. Keeping a close eye on CVR stability while pushing CTR upward will be the key combination to watch as holiday traffic increases.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $12,839 in ad spend driving $21,074 in PPC revenue, the ACOS of 60.9% indicates there is meaningful room to sharpen targeting efficiency and reduce cost-per-click waste. The priority should be tightening keyword bids on underperforming terms and reallocating budget toward the ad groups and placements already delivering the strongest returns. A disciplined bid optimization pass this week could meaningfully improve ROAS from 1.64x without sacrificing the sales momentum already built.
►
Actions This Week — Audit the top spend-heavy keywords and reduce bids on any terms running above a 70% ACOS to redirect budget toward proven converters. Test new ad creative variations — including updated main image angles and headline copy — to address the 0.38% CTR and generate more top-of-funnel clicks. With peak day landing on Monday, consider front-loading ad budgets earlier in the week to capture the same early-week demand surge in Week 47.
