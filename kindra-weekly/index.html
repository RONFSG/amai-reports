Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra closed Week 37 with $43,766 in total sales, marking a solid +3.3% week-over-week gain and continuing a positive growth trajectory heading into mid-September. A ROAS of 1.65x alongside a TACoS of 35.7% reflect a paid-heavy revenue mix where ads are driving meaningful volume but organic contribution still has room to grow relative to total spend. With Friday, September 11 emerging as the peak day, there is clear consumer engagement momentum to build on as we move into Week 38.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR of 15.14% is a strong signal that shoppers who reach the listing are converting at a healthy rate, which speaks well to product-page relevance and offer strength. The opportunity now is to feed more qualified traffic into that high-converting funnel by improving ad-level CTR, currently at 0.58%, through sharper creative and more targeted audience alignment. Keeping a close eye on the CVR-to-CTR relationship week over week will help confirm whether traffic quality improvements are translating into sustained revenue growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $15,626 in ad spend generating $25,777 in PPC revenue, the engine is working, though an ACOS of 60.6% signals that spend efficiency has meaningful room to tighten. Prioritizing budget toward the highest-converting campaigns and pulling back on underperforming placements can help bring ACOS closer to a 45–50% target range without sacrificing volume. A disciplined bid review this week, focused on keyword-level performance, will be the most direct lever to improve return on every dollar spent.
►
Actions This Week — Conduct a full keyword-level audit across active campaigns, pausing or reducing bids on terms with high spend and low conversion to work ACOS down from 60.6% toward a more efficient threshold. Test new ad creative variations on top-performing campaigns to address the 0.58% CTR, focusing on headline messaging and image selection to attract higher-intent clicks. Use Friday as a strategic anchor by ensuring budgets are not capped mid-day on September 18, given last week's peak-day pattern on Friday, September 11.
