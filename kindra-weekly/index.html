►

Incremental bid adjustments on underperforming targets, combined with protecting top converters, will be the key lever to bring ACOS down while sustaining revenue growth.
►
Actions This Week — Audit campaign-level ACOS to identify the highest-spend, lowest-return targets and reallocate that budget toward converting keywords and ASINs to improve overall efficiency.
