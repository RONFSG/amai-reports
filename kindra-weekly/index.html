►

►
Metrics to Watch — CVR held strong at 16.05%, indicating that shoppers who land on the listing are converting at a healthy rate, which is a real asset to build on. Keeping a close eye on both CTR and CVR together week-over-week will give the clearest signal of whether paid traffic quality is improving.
►

The constructive path forward is to identify the highest-spending, lowest-converting campaigns and reallocate that budget toward terms that have demonstrated stronger conversion efficiency. Tightening bid discipline across underperforming targets could meaningfully improve ROAS beyond the current 1.84x within the next two to three weeks.
