►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra generated $45,989 in total sales for Week 46 (Nov 8–14), marking a solid +4.4% week-over-week gain on 844 units sold. Momentum is building heading into Week 47, and with the holiday shopping window accelerating, conditions are favorable for continued upward movement.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR came in strong at 16.08%, signaling that shoppers who land on Kindra's listings are converting at a healthy rate, which is a real competitive advantage heading into peak season. Monday, Nov 9 was the week's highest-performing day at $45,989 in cumulative sales, confirming that early-week demand is a pattern worth leaning into with scheduling and budget decisions. Keeping a close eye on CTR (currently 0.38% from ads) will help identify where creative improvements can unlock more efficient top-of-funnel traffic.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $12,833 in ad spend driving $20,841 in PPC revenue, the ACOS of 61.6% signals an opportunity to tighten targeting and improve return on each dollar deployed. TACoS at 27.9% is a useful reminder that ad investment is supporting a broader revenue base, and incremental improvements to ACOS will have an outsized positive effect on overall profitability. Focusing budget on the highest-converting campaigns and dayparting toward early-week demand could meaningfully improve efficiency in Week 47.
►
Actions This Week — Reallocate ad budget toward top-converting campaigns and suppress or reduce bids on high-spend, low-conversion placements to work ACOS back toward a more efficient range. Test new ad creative variations — including updated headlines and imagery — to improve the 0.38% CTR and drive more qualified clicks without increasing spend. Capitalize on the strong Monday demand pattern by ensuring budgets are uncapped and bids are competitive at the start of Week 47 to maximize early-week momentum.
