Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra generated $44,307 in total sales across Week 47 (Nov 15–21), marking a solid +4.5% week-over-week increase on 909 units sold. A ROAS of 1.81x alongside a TACoS of 27.0% reflects that paid traffic is driving meaningful volume, though ad costs remain a significant portion of total revenue. Momentum is building heading into Week 48, and with Thursday Nov 19 confirmed as the peak shopping day, there is a clear opportunity to front-load spend mid-week to capture similar demand patterns.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR of 18.14% is a genuine strength and signals that shoppers landing on Kindra's listings are highly motivated to buy, which is a strong foundation to scale from. CTR of 0.53% on the ad side represents the clearest lever to pull — improving ad creative and copy to increase click-through will compound the already-strong conversion rate downstream. Keeping a close eye on how CVR holds as spend scales will confirm whether the current audience targeting is sustainable at higher volume.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $11,975 in ad spend driving $21,630 in PPC revenue, the ACOS of 55.4% indicates that for every dollar earned through ads, more than half is going back into advertising, which leaves room to tighten efficiency without sacrificing growth. The focus should shift toward identifying the highest-converting keywords and reallocating budget away from terms with elevated spend and lower return. Gradual bid adjustments on underperforming targets over the next 7–10 days can meaningfully move ACOS closer to a 45–48% target range while protecting revenue momentum.
►
Actions This Week — Increase mid-week ad budgets on Tuesday through Thursday to replicate the Thursday Nov 19 peak-day pattern and capture demand at its highest concentration point. Test new ad creative variations — including updated main image angles and headline copy — to improve the 0.53% CTR and drive more qualified traffic into the already-strong 18.14% CVR funnel. Conduct a keyword-level spend audit to pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding a 65% ACOS threshold, redirecting that budget toward proven converters to work TACoS down from 27.0% toward the 23–24% range.
