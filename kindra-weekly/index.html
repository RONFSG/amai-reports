Weekly Insights & Actions
►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra closed Week 32 with $32,014 in total sales, a solid +3.4% week-over-week gain that marks continued upward momentum heading into mid-August. A ROAS of 1.70x alongside a TACoS of 29.8% reflects that paid spend is driving meaningful volume, though ad costs remain a substantial share of total revenue, signaling room to improve organic contribution. With Sunday August 2 serving as the peak day, there is a clear opportunity to lean into weekend demand patterns and build on this trajectory into Week 33.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR of 12.70% is a genuine strength and confirms that shoppers arriving at Kindra's listings are converting at a healthy rate, which is a strong foundation to build on. CTR of 0.49% on the ad side represents the clearest lever available — even modest improvements here would expand the top of the funnel and compound the existing conversion efficiency. Keeping a close eye on both metrics together will help the team identify whether incremental spend is being deployed where it converts best.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — With $9,536 in ad spend generating $16,235 in PPC revenue, the ads are working — and an ACOS of 58.7% tells us there is meaningful upside in tightening targeting and bid allocation across campaigns. The focus this week should be on reallocating budget from underperforming keywords toward the segments that are already demonstrating strong CVR, which will naturally improve ACOS over time. Small, deliberate optimizations to bid strategy and match types can move ROAS meaningfully without requiring additional spend.
►
Actions This Week — Audit keyword-level ACOS across all active campaigns and pause or reduce bids on terms exceeding 70% ACOS, redirecting that budget toward proven converters. Test new ad creative variations — particularly headlines and imagery — to address the 0.49% CTR and drive more qualified clicks into the funnel. Given Sunday's peak performance, consider scheduling budget boosts or dayparting adjustments to capture elevated weekend demand ahead of Week 33.
