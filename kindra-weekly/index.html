►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra generated $34,164 in total sales across 709 units for Week 34 (August 16–22), marking a solid 5.9% week-over-week increase and continuing a positive growth trajectory. With Saturday August 22 closing as the peak day, momentum heading into Week 35 is strong and sets a favorable baseline to build from.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR held at a healthy 12.74%, confirming that shoppers arriving at the listing are converting at a competitive rate, which is a meaningful signal of product-market fit and listing quality. The CTR of 0.53% on ads represents the clearest near-term lever — incremental improvements here can amplify PPC revenue beyond the $18,357 recorded this week without requiring additional budget. Keeping a close eye on CVR stability as ad volume scales will help ensure conversion efficiency is preserved through Week 35.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — The team deployed $11,702 in ad spend this week, generating $18,357 in PPC revenue, and while a 63.7% ACOS signals room to sharpen targeting precision, the week-over-week sales growth confirms the spend is actively fueling demand. Tightening bids on underperforming keywords and reallocating budget toward top-converting search terms can meaningfully reduce ACOS without sacrificing the volume gains achieved this week. A focused bid optimization pass entering Week 35 positions the account to drive down TACoS from 34.3% while protecting the upward sales trend.
►
Actions This Week — Audit keyword-level ACOS across all active campaigns and pause or reduce bids on any terms exceeding a 70% ACOS threshold to reclaim budget for higher-efficiency placements. Test fresh ad creative variants — including updated main image angles and headline copy — to improve the 0.53% CTR and drive more qualified traffic into the funnel. Prioritize Saturday as a high-spend day given its confirmed peak performance, and ensure budget caps are set to avoid early day-parting cutoffs ahead of that natural demand surge.
