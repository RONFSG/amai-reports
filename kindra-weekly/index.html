►
Weekly Highlights — Kindra generated $43,892 in total sales for Week 47 (Nov 15–21), marking a solid +3.7% week-over-week increase with 931 units sold. With Thursday Nov 19 as the peak sales day and momentum building heading into the final weeks of November, the brand is well-positioned to capitalize on continued holiday demand next week.
►
Metrics to Watch — CVR came in strong at 18.59%, signaling that shoppers who reach Kindra's listings are converting at a healthy rate, which is a real competitive advantage heading into peak season. PPC revenue of $22,245 represents approximately 50.7% of total sales, confirming that paid traffic is a primary growth lever worth closely monitoring. Keeping an eye on how CVR holds as traffic scales next week will be key to sustaining efficient growth.
►
Ad Spend Efficiency — At $11,969 in ad spend with an ACOS of 53.8%, there is a clear opportunity to sharpen targeting and improve return on individual campaign placements. The gap between ACOS and an efficient target range suggests that reallocating budget toward the highest-converting keywords and pausing underperformers could meaningfully improve ROAS beyond the current 1.86x. A focused bid optimization pass this week would put the account in a stronger position for the high-traffic days ahead.
►
Actions This Week — Audit the top 20% of spend-driving keywords and tighten bids on those with ACOS above 60% to bring overall ACOS closer to a 40–45% target range. Plan a budget increase of 10–15% for the final week of November to capture incremental demand while CVR remains strong.
